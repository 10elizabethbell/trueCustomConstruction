# True Custom Construction LLC — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (index.html, photos in img/), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page marketing site)

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners near the business who need yard grading/drainage, sod prep, or a remodel, arriving from a Facebook post or group link on their phone (Facebook in-app browser).
- **Secondary:** the owner of True Custom Construction, who sees this site first and decides whether to keep it.

## Purpose
Give a business that exists only as a Facebook page a real home on the web: say what they do, show their work, and get a homeowner to ask for the free consultation they already offer.

## Positioning
A crew that brings its own heavy equipment to the job (tracked Cat 279D skid steer and a dump truck seen in their video; ownership vs. rental *inferred*, not confirmed) and does both earthwork and remodels.

## Capabilities
- Services (verbatim from their post): remodels; sod prep; grading the lawn for proper drainage; free consultation.
- Primary action: message the business on Facebook Messenger for a free consultation (the only channel they publish).
- Quote request form that composes a message the visitor can send through Messenger (and, once a phone number is known, by text).

## Constraints
- No phone, email, address, service area, hours, owner name, reviews, prices, license or insurance info published. None may be invented.
- Brand: no logo found; their Facebook profile photo is a finished dark board-and-batten house in snow.
- Must work first on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser, on cellular.

## Brand commitments
- Casual, work-crew voice ("We do remodels! Sod prep today…"). Speak as "we".
- No black page ground. No demo/sample wording.

## Evidence on hand
- One 44-second job-site video (Facebook group post, 2026-10-18): graded yard, drainage riser by the foundation, Cat 279D tracked skid steer, white dump truck. Four stills cropped from it (faces and bystanders cropped out).
- Facebook profile photo (house), used as a work photo — *inferred* to be their build.

## Principles
1. Honest over impressive: every claim traces to the brief or their own post.
2. Phone first; the first screen shows what they do and how to ask.
3. Let the visitor do the job: the hero lets you grade the dirt yourself.
